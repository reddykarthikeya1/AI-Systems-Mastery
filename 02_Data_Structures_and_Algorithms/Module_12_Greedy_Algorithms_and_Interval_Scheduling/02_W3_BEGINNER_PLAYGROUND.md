# 🐣 W3Schools-Style Playground: Greedy Algorithms

> *"Greedy means: grab the biggest piece of cake on the table right now without worrying about tomorrow."*

---

## 1. When Does Greedy Work?
Greedy works when taking the best option right now NEVER hurts your future chances (e.g. interval scheduling: finishing an event earliest leaves the maximum time for future events!).
