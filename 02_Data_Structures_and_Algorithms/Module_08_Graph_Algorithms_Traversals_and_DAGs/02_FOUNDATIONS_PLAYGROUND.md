## 1. BFS vs DFS: The Flashlight vs The String
- **BFS (Breadth-First Search)**: Turn on a flashlight. It lights up in concentric circles around you. Finds the **shortest path**!
- **DFS (Depth-First Search)**: Tie a string to your waist and walk down a dark cave until you hit a dead end, then rewind your string!
