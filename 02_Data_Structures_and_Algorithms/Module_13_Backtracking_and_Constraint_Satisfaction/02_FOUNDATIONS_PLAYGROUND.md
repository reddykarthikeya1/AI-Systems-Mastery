# 🐣 Interactive Foundations Playground: Backtracking

> *"Backtracking is trying a path in a maze: if you hit a wall, you back up to the crossroads and try another way."*

---

## 1. The 3 Steps of Backtracking:
1. **Choose**: Make a decision (add a number).
2. **Explore**: Recurse forward to see what happens.
3. **Un-choose**: Undo your decision (pop the number) so you can try other branches!
