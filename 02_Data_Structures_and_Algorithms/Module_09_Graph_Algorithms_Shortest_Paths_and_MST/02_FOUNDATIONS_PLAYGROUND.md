# 🐣 Interactive Foundations Playground: Shortest Paths

> *"Dijkstra's algorithm is just Google Maps finding you the fastest route without tolls."*

---

## 1. How Dijkstra Works in 3 Steps:
1. Always look at the cheapest place you can reach right now.
2. Step there and lock in its shortest distance.
3. Check all its neighbors: if reaching them through this new step is cheaper, update their cost!
