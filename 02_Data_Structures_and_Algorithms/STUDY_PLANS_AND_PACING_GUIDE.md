# Study Plans & Pacing Guide: DSA Mastery

- Week 3: Modules 08 - 09 (Graphs: BFS, DFS, Dijkstra, Topological Sort)
- Week 4: Modules 10 - 12 (Dynamic Programming 1D/2D, Greedy Algorithms)

- Weeks 7 - 9: Graphs & State Exploration (Modules 08 - 09, 13)
