# 🐣 Interactive Foundations Playground: LRU Cache

> *"An LRU cache is like your desk: when your desk is full of papers and you need a new one, you throw away the paper you haven't touched for the longest time."*

---

## 1. How Python's `OrderedDict` or Doubly Linked Lists Power Caches
Every time you use an item, you pick it up and put it on top of the pile. When the pile gets too tall, the item at the very bottom gets thrown away!
