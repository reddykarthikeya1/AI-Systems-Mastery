# ⏱️ Master Study Plans & Pacing Guide
### Calibrated Pacing for the 27-Module, 179-Hour Curriculum

This pacing guide organizes the **27 modules** and **7 phase checkpoints** (~179 total audited hours) into three structured learning tracks. Choose the track matching your schedule and background.

---

## 📊 Summary of Learning Tracks

| Track | Commitment | Total Duration | Weekly Hours | Best For |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **Track A: 4-Week Fast-Track** | Full-Time | 4 Weeks | ~45 hrs/week | Sabbaticals, intensive bootcamps, rapid upskilling |
| **Track B: 8-Week Professional** | Accelerated | 8 Weeks | ~22 hrs/week | Dedicated engineers, evening & weekend builders |
| **Track C: 16-Week Mastery** | Steady-Pace | 16 Weeks | ~11 hrs/week | Working professionals balancing full-time jobs |
