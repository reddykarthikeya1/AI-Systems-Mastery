# Beginner's Guide: 08. Distributed Training & Large-Scale GPU Infrastructure

Welcome to **08. Distributed Training & Large-Scale GPU Infrastructure**!

## How to Approach This Course
1. **Never Skip the First Principles**: Every module's `01_README.md` builds the architectural and mathematical intuition from scratch before touching complex code.
2. **Standard File Structure in Every Module**:
   - `01_README.md`: Deep theoretical foundation, architectural diagrams, mathematical formulations.
   - `02_PROJECT_GUIDE.md`: Specification for building the production reference engine.
   - `03_SELF_ASSESSMENT_AND_CHALLENGES.md`: Conceptual interview questions, quiz items, and Staff/Principal design prompts.
   - `04_TROUBLESHOOTING_AND_EDGE_CASES.md`: Real-world bugs, performance bottlenecks, and silent failure modes.
   - `starter/`: Hands-on practice scaffolding.
   - `project_solution/`: Verified reference implementation.
   - `debug_lab/`: Broken scenarios to diagnose and fix.
